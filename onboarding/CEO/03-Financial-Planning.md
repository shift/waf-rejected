## 🛡️ Financial Risk Management

### Revenue Risks & Mitigation

#### Customer Concentration Risk
- **Current**: Largest customer <8% of revenue
- **Target**: No customer >15% of revenue
- **Mitigation**: Diversified customer acquisition across segments

#### Churn Risk
- **Metric**: <2% monthly churn target
- **Early Warning**: Customer health scoring
- **Mitigation**: Proactive customer success, product stickiness

#### Competitive Pricing Pressure
- **Strategy**: Value-based pricing, not cost-plus
- **Differentiation**: Performance advantages justify premium
- **Flexibility**: Multiple pricing tiers, usage-based options

### Operational Risks & Controls

#### Cash Flow Management
- **Cash Runway**: 18+ months at all times
- **Burn Rate**: Monthly monitoring with quarterly forecasts
- **Working Capital**: Automated billing, collections management
- **Scenario Planning**: Best/worst case financial models

#### Gross Margin Protection
- **Infrastructure Optimization**: Auto-scaling, reserved instances
- **R&D Tax Credits**: 15-20% of engineering costs
- **Support Automation**: AI-powered support reducing costs
- **Premium Pricing**: Value-based pricing protecting margins
