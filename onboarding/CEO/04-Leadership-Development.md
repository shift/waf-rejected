## 💪 Culture & Values Framework

### Core Values

#### 1. Customer Obsession
- **Principle**: Customer success drives our success
- **Behaviors**: Customer feedback prioritization, user experience focus
- **Metrics**: NPS >50, CSAT >4.7/5, customer retention >95%

#### 2. Technical Excellence  
- **Principle**: Best-in-class technology and engineering practices
- **Behaviors**: Code quality, performance optimization, security-first
- **Metrics**: 99.95% uptime, <5ms latency, zero security incidents

#### 3. Ownership Mentality
- **Principle**: Act like an owner, think long-term
- **Behaviors**: Accountability, initiative, results-oriented
- **Metrics**: Goal achievement, project delivery, quality outcomes

#### 4. Continuous Learning
- **Principle**: Always growing, improving, and innovating
- **Behaviors**: Skill development, feedback seeking, knowledge sharing
- **Metrics**: Training hours, certification rates, innovation projects

#### 5. Inclusive Excellence
- **Principle**: Diverse perspectives create better outcomes
- **Behaviors**: Respectful communication, inclusive decision-making
- **Metrics**: Diversity hiring, inclusion surveys, employee satisfaction

### Performance Management System

#### Quarterly OKRs (Objectives & Key Results)
- **Company-level OKRs**: CEO sets 3-5 company objectives
- **Team-level OKRs**: Each team aligns with company objectives
- **Individual OKRs**: Personal objectives tied to team/company goals
- **Review Process**: Monthly check-ins, quarterly reviews

#### Annual Performance Reviews
- **360-Degree Feedback**: Manager, peers, direct reports
- **Competency Assessment**: Role-specific skills evaluation
- **Goal Achievement**: OKR completion and quality metrics
- **Career Development**: Growth planning and advancement paths

#### Compensation Philosophy
- **Market Competitive**: 75th percentile for top performers
- **Equity Participation**: Meaningful ownership for all employees
- **Performance-Based**: Merit increases tied to performance
- **Transparent Bands**: Clear compensation levels and progression
