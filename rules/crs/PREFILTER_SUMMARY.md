## Soundness contract

For every gated entry, a match requires at least one listed literal in the transformed value. The prefilter scans the raw body plus the extraction variants (URL-decode, JSON-unescape, HTML-entity-decode) and every supported transform pipeline. A miss therefore proves the rule cannot match a body-derived value. Any entry listed as ungated forces full CRS evaluation.
